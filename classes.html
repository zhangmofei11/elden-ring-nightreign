Recommended Co-op Teams

Balanced Team

Vagabond (Tank) + Samurai (DPS) + Confessor (Healer). Solid all-around composition.

Glass Cannon

Astrologer + Bandit + Samurai. High damage but requires skilled players.

Beginner Friendly

Warrior + Confessor + Vagabond. Forgiving mistakes, easy to coordinate.
