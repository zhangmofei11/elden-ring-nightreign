Build Tips

Early Game

Focus on Vigor first. Survival is key in co-op. Aim for 20-25 Vigor before investing in damage stats.

Mid Game

Balance your primary damage stat with Endurance. Don't neglect Mind if using spells frequently.

Late Game

Specialize fully in your primary stat. +8 weapons scale better with focused stats than spread分配.
