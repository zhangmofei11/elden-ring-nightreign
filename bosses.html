Boss Guide

Complete boss strategies with attack patterns and weakness guides.

Scout Boss

Night 1 - Tutorial Boss

HP800
Easy

Attack Patterns

Slash Combo

2-hit combo. Dodge sideways after first hit.

Charge Attack

Long wind-up. Back dodge to avoid.

Strategy

This is a tutorial boss. Stay patient, learn controls. Attack 1-2 times after his combos. No special mechanics.

Tree Guardian

Night 2 - Forest Boss

HP2,200
Normal

Attack Patterns

Root Sweep

Ground roots attack. Jump to avoid.

Branch Slam

Area denial. Run to edge of arena.

Summon Thorns

Spawns thorn patches. Stay moving.

Strategy

Stay mobile. Attack during recovery after Branch Slam. Fire damage effective. Watch for root attacks from below.

The Lord of Night

Night 5 - Final Boss

HP8,000
Extreme

Attack Patterns

Night Flame

Wide AoE fire explosion. Run to safe zones.

Shadow Clone

Creates 2 clones. Focus on real boss.

Night's Doom

Full screen attack. Hide behind pillars.

Phase 2 - Dark Nova

Rapid consecutive attacks. Survive for 20s.

Strategy

• Co-op is essential for this fight. Coordinate with team.

• Use pillars for cover during Night's Doom.

• Ignore shadow clones - they disappear after 10s.

• Phase 2: Split responsibilities. One player draws aggro, others deal damage.

• Holy damage effective in Phase 2.

Recommended Level: 40-50 | Recommended Setup: Full team with healing class
