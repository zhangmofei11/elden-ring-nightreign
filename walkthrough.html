Complete Walkthrough

Stage-by-stage guide with objectives, collectibles, and boss strategies.

I

Night 1: The Awakening

Starting Area → First Trials

Tutorial
Duration:30-45 min
Collectibles:3/8
Difficulty:Easy

Objectives:

  • • Select your class and customize loadout
  • • Learn basic combat controls
  • • Find the first Rune Well
  • • Defeat the Scout Boss (mini-boss)
  • • Reach the Night's End Altar
II

Night 2: The Deep Woods

Forest Region → Underground Springs

Main Story
Duration:1-1.5 hours
Collectibles:5/12
Difficulty:Normal

Objectives:

  • • Navigate the Misty Forest
  • • Activate the Ancient Lamp shrine
  • • Find the hidden Warrior's Ashes
  • • Defeat the Tree Guardian (mid-boss)
  • • Discover the underground hot springs
III

Night 3: The Crimson Fortress

Fortress Exterior → Inner Sanctum

Main Story
Duration:1.5-2 hours
Collectibles:7/15
Difficulty:Hard

Objectives:

  • • Breach the fortress gates
  • • Clear the Castle Barracks
  • • Find the Knight's Remembrance
  • • Defeat the Commander (mini-boss)
  • • Reach the Chapel of Anticipation
IV

Night 4: The Ashen Hollow

Volcanic Region → Dragon's Peak

Side Path
Duration:1-1.5 hours
Collectibles:4/10
Difficulty:Normal

Objectives:

  • • Cross the Cinder Marshes
  • • Find the Ancient Dragon's memory
  • • Optional: Free the trapped spirit
  • • Defeat the Ash Wraith (stealth section)
V

Night 5: The Night's End

Final Fortress → Lord of Night

Final
Duration:2-3 hours
Collectibles:6/18
Difficulty:Very Hard

Objectives:

  • • Scale the Night's Spire
  • • Defeat the Three Guardians (boss rush)
  • • Collect the Night's Blessing
  • • Face the Lord of Night (final boss)
  • • Complete the Night
