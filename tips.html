Tips & Tricks

Advanced strategies and hidden secrets to maximize your runs.

Beginner Tips

1

Stay with Your Team

Nightreign is designed for co-op. Don't wander off alone - stick with your team for easier boss fights.

2

Upgrade Rune Wells Early

Prioritize finding and activating Rune Wells in each Night. They provide permanent stat boosts.

3

Learn Dodge Timing

Perfect dodges give you brief slow-motion windows. Practice this in early Nights before harder content.

4

Use Consumables

Don't hoard items. Use your flasks and consumables liberally - you respawn at the start of each Night.

Advanced Strategies

Co-op Tanking

In co-op, one player should focus on drawing boss aggro while others deal damage. Use shield abilities to hold attention.

Best: Vagabond class

Status Effects

Bleed and Frost build up on enemies. Stack these with multiple players using same status weapons for bonus damage.

Best: Samurai + Bandit combo

Night Cycle Management

Each Night gets progressively harder. Don't waste resources early - save your best items for Nights 4-5.

Strategy

Secret Areas

Check every corner and behind destructible walls. Secret areas often contain rare equipment and Rune Wells.

Exploration

Hidden Secrets

Hidden Merchant

In Night 2, bomb the wall near the forest entrance. Secret merchant sells exclusive items.

Double Rune

Standing in certain circles doubles Rune pickup. Look for glowing symbols on the ground.

Secret Ending

Collect all 8 Memory Fragments + spare the Lord of Night once. Unlocks alternate ending.
